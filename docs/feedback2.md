**2\. ABOUT US**

Headline: Who We Are  
Introductory Text: The Agricultural Consultative Forum is Zambia's premier multi-stakeholder platform dedicated to fostering inclusive, evidence-based agricultural policy dialogue. Formed out of the vital need to bridge the gap between policy formulation and practical implementation in 1998, our mandate is to provide a multi-stakeholder platform for agricultural policy dialogue and coordination, and fostering of Public-Private Partnerships (PPPs) in Zambia’s agricultural. We serve as a neutral broker and catalyst, transforming divergent sector interests into unified national strategies that drive agricultural growth, rural development, and economic transformation.

VISION & MISSION

* Vision: A leading policy advisory organization in the formulation and development of agricultural sector policies” in Zambia.  
* Mission: To promote evidence-based policy analysis, knowledge management, and structured policy dialogue among diverse stakeholders.

**3\. OUR WORK**

Headline: Advancing Zambia's Agricultural Agenda  
Introductory Text: The distinctive competence of the Forum lies in our absolute neutrality, high-level convening power, and trusted relationships across the entire agricultural landscape. Unlike individual interest groups, we do not lobby for a single agenda; instead, we build national consensus. Our unique ability to bring agricultural stakeholders (private sector, public sector, agribusiness farmer representatives, private public sector partnerships) and international donors ensures that the policies we influence are both practical on the ground and viable at the macroeconomic level.

 \[CORE ADVOCACY PILLARS\]  
**Pillar 1: Policy Advocacy and Reform**: We identify policy bottlenecks, analyze legislative impacts, and advocate for regulatory reforms that improve the agricultural business environment.  
**Pillar 2\. Multi-Stakeholder Dialogue**: We convene national and regional forums, workshops, and roundtables to build consensus on critical agricultural issues.  
**Pillar 3\. Evidence-Based Policy Analysis**: We generate data-driven insights and policy briefs to guide government decision-making and private sector investments.  
**Pillar 4\. Capacity Building**: We strengthen the analytical and advocacy capacities of farmer organizations, civil society groups, and local value-chain actors.
